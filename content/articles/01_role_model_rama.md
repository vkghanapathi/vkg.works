  exile, his battle against Ravana, and his eventual return to Ayodhya. Rama's story
  is central to Hindu ethics and provides a framework for understanding ideal behavior,
  familial relationships, and righteous governance. This article likely delves into
  the specific aspects of Rama's character and actions that have made him a timeless
  role model in Indian culture.
