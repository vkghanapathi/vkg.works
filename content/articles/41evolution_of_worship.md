abstract: This article by Dr. Vamshi Krishna Ghanapāṭhī examines the historical development
  of Hindu worship practices. It likely traces the evolution of ritual forms, deities,
  and devotional attitudes from the Vedic period to later traditions, offering insights
  into the changing religious landscape of India.
preamble: Hindu worship encompasses a diverse range of practices, from elaborate Vedic
  fire sacrifices to devotional offerings at temples and personal acts of prayer.
  The evolution of these practices reflects the dynamic interplay between Vedic traditions,
  Upanishadic thought, and the emergence of distinct sectarian movements centered
  on deities such as Vishnu, Shiva, and Shakti. Understanding this evolution requires
  careful consideration of textual sources, archaeological evidence, and the ongoing
  transformations within Hindu society.
