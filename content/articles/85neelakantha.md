abstract: This article by Dr. Vamshi Krishna Ghanapāṭhī likely presents and discusses
  the Neelakantha Stotram, a hymn dedicated to Lord Shiva in his aspect as Neelakantha,
  the blue-throated one. Scholarly analysis of stotras contributes to a deeper understanding
  of devotional practices, theological attributes, and the literary conventions within
  the Hindu tradition.
preamble: The Neelakantha Stotram belongs to the broader genre of stotras, devotional
  hymns prevalent in Hinduism. These hymns are composed to praise and invoke deities,
  often recited during worship, festivals, or personal devotion. Lord Shiva, as Neelakantha,
  is revered for his compassion in consuming the poison that arose during the churning
  of the cosmic ocean, saving the universe from destruction. The stotra tradition
  provides valuable insights into the diverse expressions of Hindu devotion and the
  multifaceted nature of its deities.
