  sage Valmiki and revered as the Adi Kavya, or first poem. It narrates the life and
  adventures of Rama, an incarnation of Vishnu, his exile, and his eventual triumph
  over the demon king Ravana. The Ramayana is not only a literary masterpiece but
  also a source of moral and spiritual guidance, shaping cultural values and inspiring
  devotion across India and beyond. Dr. Ghanapāṭhī's series likely aims to make this
  complex and influential text more accessible to a wider audience.
