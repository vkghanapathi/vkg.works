preamble: Nityakarma refers to the daily rituals and duties that are considered obligatory
  for certain individuals, primarily Brahmins, in the Hindu tradition. These practices
  are designed to purify the mind and body, foster spiritual growth, and maintain
  harmony with the divine. Rooted in the Vedas and elaborated in subsequent texts
  such as the Smritis and Puranas, Nityakarma encompasses a range of activities, including
  morning ablutions, Sandhyavandanam (twilight prayers), deity worship, and scriptural
  study. Proper execution of Nityakarma is believed to ensure well-being and pave
  the path towards liberation.
