preamble: Sandhya Upasana is a daily ritual performed by initiated members of the
  Brahmin community, rooted in the Vedic tradition. It involves the recitation of
  specific mantras, performance of symbolic gestures (mudras), and offering of water
  (arghya) to deities, particularly Gayatri. This practice is traditionally performed
  at dawn, noon, and dusk, marking the junctures of the day. The ritual is considered
  a vital component of maintaining spiritual purity and upholding dharma, as prescribed
  in the scriptures.
