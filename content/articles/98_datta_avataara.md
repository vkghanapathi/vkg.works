preamble: Dattatreya is a Hindu deity considered an embodiment of the Trimurti—Brahma,
  Vishnu, and Shiva. He is revered as a divine teacher and an epitome of spiritual
  wisdom. The concept of Datta Avatara refers to the various incarnations or manifestations
  of Dattatreya, each representing a specific aspect or purpose. Understanding these
  avatars is crucial for devotees seeking spiritual guidance and liberation, as Dattatreya
  is believed to grant boons and remove obstacles.
