His Holiness **Sri Ganapathi Sachchidananda Swamiji** is the founder and head of the
**Avadhūta Datta Peeṭham** at Mysore, a centre of the Dattatreya tradition with a
worldwide community of devotees. Revered as a spiritual master, he has guided countless
seekers through teaching, worship, music and selfless service.

He is the author and compiler of a large body of publications issued through the Peeṭham
(under the Rāgaragiṇī Trust). These range across the principal scriptures and their
exposition — the Bhagavad-Gītā with his concise Laghu-vyākhyā, renderings of the
Yoga-Vāsiṣṭha, the Devī Bhāgavatam, the Harivaṁśa and the Bhāgavata; the hymns and
narratives of the Śrī Vidyā and Devī traditions; works on Dattatreya, Hanumān, Gaṇeśa
and the Purāṇic deities; primers in Vedānta and nīti; and books for children. His
writings appear in Telugu, Kannada and English to reach devotees across communities.

He is especially celebrated as a master of **devotional music** — his Bhajana-yoga,
Bhajanamālā, Kṛtimaṇimālā and other musical compositions have carried the path of bhakti
through song to audiences across the world, and his work on the healing power of nāda is
widely known. His humanitarian initiatives include hospitals, education and the noted
**Shuka Vana** avian sanctuary for the rehabilitation of parrots and endangered birds.

Through his prolific writing, music and service, Sri Swamiji has made the wisdom of the
tradition accessible and living for a global devotee community.
