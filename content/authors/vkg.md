Dr. Vaṁśīkṛṣṇa Ghanapāṭhī is a traditionally trained Vedic scholar and a Ghanapāṭhī —
one who has mastered the ghana, the most intricate of the elaborate (vikṛti) modes of
Vedic recitation. Standing in the unbroken oral tradition of the Veda while engaging
fully with modern scholarship and technology, he has devoted his work to the
preservation, critical editing and exposition of the Vedic corpus.

As founder of **Veda Vijñāna Viṣṭāram (VVV)** in Mysore, and through his long association
with the Veda department of the Avadhūta Datta Peeṭham, he has produced an extensive
series of research-grade publications: critical editions of the Prātiśākhyas and Śikṣā
texts that govern Vedic recitation; the Padapāṭhas and Anukramaṇīs (scholarly indices)
of the Ṛgveda and the Taittirīya corpus; an original Bhāṣya on the Śrī Rudram; sa-bhāṣya
editions of Brāhmaṇa and Gṛhyasūtra texts with commentary and translation; and editions
in the śāstra literature, including the Jaiminīya Nyāyamālā Vistara of Pūrva Mīmāṁsā and
works of Vyākaraṇa.

His scholarship extends into the computational and lexical study of the Veda. He guides
the **Vaidika Pada Kosha** project — a lexicon of the unique words of the Vedic
pada-pāṭha — together with a programme of preparing critical pada-pāṭhas of the Vedic
Saṁhitās, a rule-based pada-pāṭha processing engine, and tools for generating and
verifying the vikṛti recitations. In this way the oral science of recitation and the
modern science of computation are brought to bear on one another.

Through his editions, commentaries, national scholarly seminars (Veda-Vidvat and
Śāstra-Vākyārtha sabhās) and ongoing research programmes, Dr. Ghanapāṭhī works toward the
documentation, accreditation and living continuity of the Vedic tradition.
