  of the Vedas.
preamble: The Vedas are a vast collection of hymns, prayers, and ritual instructions
  that form the foundation of Hinduism. They are considered to be apauruṣeya, or of
  non-human origin, and are traditionally transmitted orally from guru to disciple.
  The study of the Vedas is considered to be a sacred duty, and it is believed to
  lead to both material and spiritual well-being. Vedic rituals, such as the Yajna,
  are performed to invoke the blessings of the gods and to maintain cosmic order.
