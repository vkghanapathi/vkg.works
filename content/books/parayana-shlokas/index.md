abstract: This collection, *Pārāyaṇa Ślokas*, compiled by Vamśīkṛṣṇa Ghanapāṭhī, presents
  a compilation of sacred verses intended for recitation (pārāyaṇa). The work encompasses
  a variety of stotras (hymns) and ślokas (verses) dedicated to various deities within
  the Hindu pantheon, including Dattātreya, Lakṣmī, Gaṇapati, Śiva, Viṣṇu, and Hanumān.
  It includes prayers for specific times of day and activities, such as morning, bathing,
  meals, leaving the house, and before sleep. The collection also features stotras
  for protection from diseases and planetary influences, as well as verses attributed
  to figures like Śankarācārya. The text provides a resource for devotees seeking
  to engage in the practice of reciting sacred verses for spiritual benefit.
