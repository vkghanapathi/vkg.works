abstract: This book, *Rudra Ekādaśinī*, by Vamśīkṛṣṇa Ghanapāṭhī, delves into the
  Vedic ritual of Rudra Ekādaśinī. It elucidates the significance and performance
  of this complex ritual, which involves the recitation of the Rudram eleven times.
  The text provides a detailed explanation of the various components of the ritual,
  including the offerings, mantras, and the roles of the priests. It further explores
  the deeper meaning and symbolism associated with each element of the ritual, connecting
  it to broader Vedic philosophical concepts. The author draws upon traditional knowledge
  and scriptural sources to offer a comprehensive understanding of the Rudra Ekādaśinī,
  making it accessible to both scholars and practitioners interested in Vedic rituals.
