abstract: This commemorative volume marks the silver jubilee of a Veda Pāṭhaśālā (Vedic
  school), offering a multifaceted exploration of Vedic knowledge and practice. It
  includes prayers, discussions on the role of the Vedas in dharma and career planning
  for Veda students, and detailed analyses of Vedic linguistics, including the formation
  of words, their expression, and the significance of Swaras (musical notes) in Vedic
  recitation. Several articles delve into specific Vedic rituals such as Ishti and
  Saavitragnichayanamu, along with explanations of Vidhi-Sraddha. The volume also
  introduces key concepts from various Śāstras, including Nirukta, Lakshana Sastra,
  and Tarka Sastra, providing insights into Vedic interpretation and philosophy. Noteworthy
  is a section dedicated to the importance of Ghana in Vedic recitation, along with
  discussions on Anadhyana Prakaranam and philosophical concepts like 'Anando Brahma'
  and 'Tatwamasi'. The book concludes with reflections on the influence of the Vedas
  on literature and practical guidance for teachers and students of the Vedas.
