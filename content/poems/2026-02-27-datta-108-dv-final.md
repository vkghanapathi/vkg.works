abstract: Sri Datta Śatakam is a devotional hymnic composition of one hundred verses
  (śatakam) in Sanskrit dedicated to Dattātreya, the unified form of the Hindu trinity
  (Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva). Composed by Dr. Vamshi Krishna Ghanapāṭhī and reissued on
  the occasion of Dattātreya Jayantī in 2011, this work systematically expounds the
  philosophical and theological attributes of Dattātreya as the supreme guru and embodiment
  of non-dual Brahman, addressing his incarnational purpose, nomenclature, transcendent
  nature, and role as world-teacher.
preamble: This work belongs to the Sanskrit devotional and philosophical tradition
  of Advaita Vedānta, specifically within the Dattātreya sampradāya (lineage). Dattātreya,
  venerated as an avatāra and the embodiment of the spiritual master (guru) principle,
  occupies a central place in Hindu tantric and yogic traditions. The composition
  draws upon Vedic references, Upaniṣadic philosophy, and the devotional idiom to
  articulate Dattātreya's significance as a liberating principle and universal teacher.
  The text is framed within a guru-disciple relationship, dedicating the work to the
  author's spiritual preceptor, Śrī Gaṇapati Saccidānanda Svāmī, and invoking his
  blessings for the successful articulation of Dattātreya's glory.

translation_en: 'Dedicated with Divine Blessings of

  Paramapujya Sri Ganapati Sachchidananda Swamiji,

  The presiding Deity of Avadhuta Datta Peetham

  Sri Datta Shatakam

  Reprinted on the grand occasion of Dattatreya Jayanti in the year Khara.


  Submission:

  May that great preceptor, Sri Ganapati Yamini Sachchidananda Datta, whose effulgent
  wisdom illuminates the nature of Dattatreya, who pervades all beings, and who even
  now stirs my dull intellect to clarity, inspire the wellspring of my speech. I adore
  that Brahman-abiding one.


  May Vamshikrishna, desiring to attain the wisdom born of the grace of the Self,
  offer and adorn the lotus feet of the Guru with this garland of a hundred jewel-like
  verses, acquired through long service to the Guru.


  Avatāroddeshaḥ (Purpose of the Incarnation)


  1.  I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who incarnated on earth, the peerless one, for the upliftment of all people,
  and who manifested Himself to proclaim the glory of the Guru''s state.


  2.  I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who was revealed to Anasuya and Atri, ascetics renowned for their incomparable
  glory and vows, on the Riksha mountain, to bestow grace upon the world, and who
  was born as Atri''s son on the full moon of Margashirsha, for the establishment
  of auspiciousness in the world.


  3.  I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who accepted the name Dattatreya, supremely established and skilled in benefiting
  the world, ever capable and foremost in protecting devotees at various times, and
  delightful, auspicious, and purifying to those who praise Him with Vedic utterances.


  4.  I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, the mere remembrance of whose most excellent name reduces heaps of sins to
  ashes, whose most beloved prayer generates supreme and unprecedented merit, and
  whose most profound awakening, when contemplated, carries one across the great ocean
  of worldly existence.


  5.  I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who is greater than the creators, generating the sentiments of wisdom and
  detachment in the minds of those who take refuge in Him, who is greater than the
  controllers, bestowing countless riches upon those who bow down to Him, and who
  is greater than the givers, being most potent in destroying the desires of the mind.


  6.  I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who, as the eternal and true consciousness, the substratum of the world,
  the non-dual reality, proclaims this principle: that the one Sat Brahman manifests
  as countless jivas, being the all-pervading Self, and who delights in the company
  of dogs, symbolizing the Vedas.


  7.  I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who, as the three-formed deity, reveals the nature of Hari with the lotus
  and the chakra in His hands, who holds the trident and the damaru, granting fearlessness,
  embodying the nature of Shambhu, and who bears the marks of Brahma, with the rosary,
  water pot, and sacred thread, ever engaged in the repetition of the mantra.


  8.  I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who, seeing the six changes (bhāva vikāra) that appear in the world due to
  superimposition, which are effects of Maya and cause the illusion of reality through
  experience, removes those changes, being Himself unchanging.


  9.  I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, for whom, if He were to give, these two hands are insufficient to receive,
  who grants liberation from bondage to the desireless, and fulfills all desires to
  those with desires. What more can I say? He, full of compassion, bestows the great
  bliss of Sachchidananda.


  10. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who initiated Himself into the state of Avadhuta, renouncing the bonds of
  honor, home, wife, children, cattle, house, heaven, fame, and other desires, for
  the sake of knowledge and detachment for countless devotees, and who is the light
  of the essence of the Vedas, like a creeper.


  11. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who, by superimposing the signs of wisdom such as humility, made these twenty-four
  entities His Gurus, and thus revealed the greatness of the world-teacher, illuminating
  the auspicious path.


  12. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who, when all people were being burned and tormented in the fire of worldly
  existence, appeared to King Yadu, tranquil in Self, like an elephant immersed in
  the waters of the Ganges, and who, being the ocean of wisdom, was earnestly requested
  to impart the secret of boundless joy.


  13. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who teaches that this threefold world, consisting of objects, qualities,
  and actions, is to be understood as distinct lessons, each reciting its own part,
  and who, through subtle observation, experiences bliss, realizing, "I am not the
  body."


  14. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who, speaking of the Earth as the first and foremost Guru, who endures being
  trampled upon, is steadfast, bears burdens, and gives her all for the sake of others,
  taught the essence of forbearance and selfless service.


  15. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who taught that the Wind is my Guru, benefiting the entire world through
  contact with all things, carrying odors yet remaining detached, and separating the
  good from the bad, thus revealing the essence of non-attachment.


  16. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who, being all-pervading like the sky, yet appearing different due to pots,
  monasteries, and bodies, is vast, immeasurable, and without beginning or end, and
  who declared, "The Self is infinite," thus revealing the nature of the Self.


  17. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who taught that the Waters are my Gurus, shining, compassionate, pure, desirable,
  colorless, gentle, and the essence of taste, and that one should attain purity like
  them, thus revealing the essence of purity.


  18. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who taught that Fire, with its seven flames, consuming all, accepts even
  the position of my Guru, and, being radiant, burns away all impurities and destroys
  all faults, thus revealing the essence of purification.


  19. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who taught that the Moon, adorned with marks, accepts the position of my
  Guru through its waxing and waning, and that the body bears birth and death, ever
  proceeding towards death, thus revealing that the seen is perishable, but the Self
  is eternal.


  20. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who taught that the Sun is my Guru, visible daily with its various rays,
  appearing different in pots, rivers, lakes, and waterfalls, yet remaining non-dual,
  and that the Self resides in various beings, thus revealing the essence of unity
  in diversity.


  21. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who taught that the Bird is my Guru, whose sweet sounds led to its death
  at the hands of a hunter, and who, seeing his wife caught in the net with her young
  ones, was overcome with sorrow, thus revealing that delusion leads to sorrow, and
  one should awaken to discrimination.


  22. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who taught that the Ocean, in whose deep womb countless jewels and immeasurable
  treasures exist, remains calm and uniform, whether the streams flowing into it are
  small or large, thus revealing the essence of depth and tranquility.


  23. I adore that Brahman-abiding Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my
  heart, who taught that the Python, content with whatever comes its way, whether
  little, delicious, or abundant, remains happy, lying in wait, and that one should
  be content with what is obtained, thus revealing the essence of contentment.'

  అవతారోద్దేశః (అవతరణ ఉద్దేశం)


  1. సకల జనముల నుద్ధరించుటకు భూమిపై అవతరించిన అద్వితీయుడు, గురుపద మహిమను తెలుపుటకు
  ఆవిర్భవించిన వాడు, ముల్లోకములను జనన మరణ చక్రము నుండి విడిపించువాడు ఐన సచ్చిదానంద
  దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  2. ఋక్ష పర్వతమున తపస్సు చేయుచున్న అనసూయ, అత్రి మహామునులకు లోకమును అనుగ్రహించుటకు
  ఇష్టుడై వెల్లడి అయినవాడు, మార్గశీర్ష పౌర్ణమి నాడు అత్రికి పుత్రుడుగా జన్మించి శుభములను
  స్థాపించిన సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  3. లోకోపకారమునకై దత్తాత్రేయ అను పేరును పొందినవాడు, భక్తులను రక్షించుటకు సమర్థుడు,
  బ్రహ్మవాదుల స్తుతికి సంతోషించువాడు, పవిత్రుడు ఐన సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  4. ఏ నామమును స్మరించిన పాపరాశి భస్మమగునో, ఏ ప్రార్థన గొప్ప పుణ్యకర్మలను కలుగచేయునో,
  ఏ ప్రబోధ సంసార సాగరమునుండి తరింపచేయునో, అట్టి సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  5. సృష్టికర్తలకంటే గొప్పవాడు, ఆశ్రయించినవారికి జ్ఞాన వైరాగ్యములను కలిగించువాడు,
  నియమించువారికంటే గొప్పవాడు, నమస్కరించినవారికి లెక్కలేనన్ని సంపదలను ఇచ్చువాడు, మనస్సును
  జయించుటకు సమర్థుడు ఐన సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  6. నిత్య సత్య చైతన్యము, జగత్తుకు ఆధారము, అద్వితీయము, సర్వాత్మకమైన పరబ్రహ్మము జీవుల
  కోటిలో ఉన్నదని చెప్పువాడు, వేదస్వరూపులైన కుక్కలతో ఆనందించు సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా
  హృదయమున భజింతును.


  7. చేతులలో పద్మము, చక్రము ధరించి విష్ణు స్వరూపుడు, త్రిశూలము, ఢమరుకము, అభయముద్ర
  ధరించి శివుడు, జపమాల, కమండలము, యజ్ఞోపవీతము ధరించి బ్రహ్మ స్వరూపుడు ఐన సచ్చిదానంద
  దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  8. జగత్తులో కనిపించే ఆరు మార్పులు (పుట్టుట, ఉండుట, పెరుగుట, మారుట, తరుగుట, నశించుట)
  మాయా కార్యములు, అనుభవము వలన సత్యమువలె భ్రమను కలిగించును. వాటిని తొలగించువాడు, వికారము
  లేనివాడు ఐన సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  9. ఇవ్వడానికి సిద్ధంగా ఉంటే, తీసుకోవడానికి రెండు చేతులు చాలవు, నిష్కాములకు బంధవిముక్తిని,
  కాముకులకు కోరికలను తీర్చువాడు, దయతో సచ్చిదానందమును పంచువాడు ఐన సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని
  నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  10. జ్ఞాన వైరాగ్యముల కొరకు భక్తుల బంధములను తెంచువాడు, అవధూత దీక్షను స్వీకరించినవాడు,
  వేదముల సారమును ప్రకాశింపచేయు సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  11. వినయము మొదలైన జ్ఞాన చిహ్నములతో ఇరువది నాలుగు మందిని గురువులుగా స్వీకరించి, జగత్తులో
  గురువు యొక్క గొప్పతనాన్ని తెలియజేసిన సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  12. సంసారమనే అగ్నిలో కాలుతున్న ప్రజలను చూసి, గంగానదిలో మునిగిన ఏనుగు వలె శాంతంగా
  ఉన్న యదురాజుకు జ్ఞానోపదేశం చేసిన సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  13. ఈ ముల్లోకములు హేయమైనవి, ఉపాదేయమైనవి అను భావనను కలిగించును. ప్రతి వస్తువు తన
  స్వభావాన్ని తెలుపుతూ బోధించును. సూక్ష్మ దృష్టితో పరిశీలించి ఆనందమును అనుభవించువాడు,
  నేను శరీరమును కాను అని తెలుపు సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.


  14. భూమి గురువు, తనను త్రొక్కినను సహించును, భారమును మోయును, ఇతరుల కొరకు తన సర్వస్వాన్ని
  ఇచ్చును, ఓర్పు కలిగి ఉండాలని బోధించిన సచ్చిదానంద దత్తుని నా హృదయమున భజింతును.
