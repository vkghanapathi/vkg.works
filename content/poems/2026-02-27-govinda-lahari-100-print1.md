abstract: Govinda Laharī is a devotional lyric poem composed by Vamshi Krishna Ghanapāṭhī,
  structured in the śikhariṇī metre and dedicated to Lord Viṣṇu (Govinda). The work
  comprises devotional verses celebrating divine attributes, cosmic functions, and
  philosophical concepts of non-duality, arranged thematically around epithets and
  manifestations of the supreme deity. Composed during the author's tenure as an examiner
  of Vedic studies at Tirupati, the work presents a synthesis of bhakti sentiment
  with Vedantic philosophy.
preamble: This work belongs to the śrīvaiṣṇava devotional tradition, a major Vedantic
  school venerating Lord Viṣṇu as the supreme Brahman. The composition situates itself
  within the exegetical and devotional culture of Tirupati (Śrīvēṅkaṭāchal), one of
  South India's foremost pilgrimage centres. The text draws on classical Sanskrit
  poetic conventions while employing recurring refrains (particularly the refrain
  'svvasiddhāṁ Govindo dvayrahita saṁsthāṁ pradishatām') to reinforce theological
  assertions regarding Viṣṇu's transcendent nature, salvific function, and manifestation
  in the cosmos. The work was composed under the spiritual guidance of Śrī Gaṇapati
  Saccidānanda Svāmi and addresses the glory (vaikunta) of the Lord through structured
  meditation and praise.

translation_en: 'Govinda Lahari


  Foreword: By the grace of Lord Śrīnivāsa, this person, appointed as an examiner
  of Vedic students in Śrī Veṅkaṭācala Kṣetra (Tirumala Hills), experienced the bliss
  of residing on the Adhigiri during the Navarātri season in the month of Māgha. It
  is there, in the presence of the venerable Śrī Śrī Ganapati Sachchidananda Swamiji,
  my noble preceptor, that this poetic composition was initiated by the divine will
  of Śrīpati Purushottama, with the intention of describing the cascade of His glories.
  I humbly submit this brief poem, composed in the Śikhariṇī meter, dedicated to the
  contemplation of the supreme majesty of Śrīpati, praying that it may find favor
  with the learned and thereby attain fulfillment.


  Addicted to the nectar of Hari''s name - Vamśīkṛṣṇa Ghanapāṭhī


  1.  Indescribable,

  What offering can I make of these words at the feet of Hari,

  When the universe, vast as the sky, is His domain for me to roam?

  What aspect of the Supreme Brahman, my very foundation, can I dissect,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  2.  Pathik Sneha (Friendship of a Traveler)

  If he, desiring fortune, exerted himself with such fervor,

  His friendship again made him desire fortune.

  When the path becomes smooth for us both, happiness arises with friendship,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  3.  The Cause of Recovering the Vedas

  In ancient times, when the waters were diminished by the power of demons,

  He recovered the Vedas, revealing His true essence.

  To grant the object of contemplation to intellects, or to sever the cycle of birth,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  4.  Śrīvāsa

  He, Śrīvāsa, bears fortune and possesses immense affection for others,

  Fortune cherishes his affection exceedingly from his radiant face.

  He is revered by throngs of deities with celebrated glories as ''He who is adorned
  with Śrīvatsa'',

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  5.  The Cause of Merit

  Having unceasingly studied the four Vedas, you are virtuous,

  May He, the One, indicate the deed you have done, and declare it.

  O friend, know that He is the most meritorious, dwelling in the midst of the world,

  May Govinda bestow His own self-realized, non-dual state.


  6.  The Goal of Paths

  The paths, dense with various forms of great delusion, dissolve

  In this Supreme Brahman, in His vast, majestic essence, in the eternal abode.

  Wander through the world, and ultimately rest at the lotus feet of the destroyer
  of Madhu,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  7.  Not Beloved by a Dark Complexion

  If He is dark like a cloud, and a connoisseur of beauty,

  Then how can the dark color be the most enchanting?

  He is dear because of His power of attraction, not because of His color,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  8.  The Golden Vimana

  What does the golden vimana constantly teach here?

  These feelings are not easily measurable in the infinite and finite.

  There is neither increase nor decrease in the beautiful radiance of the treasure
  of His own Self,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  9.  Abandonment of All Dharmas

  Abandon dharma and adharma, for He says, "Take refuge in Me."

  Do nothing; if your mind is fixed that the Lord will protect you,

  Without laziness, worship the Lord, constantly, with a mind dedicated to self-surrender,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  10. The Delusion of Beauty

  This beautiful-browed, slender woman generates joy and delightful infatuation,

  Or a youthful, affectionate man, or both, again, are fond of youthful pleasures.

  Attain the Lord of all beauty, immeasurable, with your mind,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  11. Residence on the Bosom of Śrīdevī and Bhūdevī

  Lakshmi and the Earth are the first to grant life,

  He causes all people to traverse the path of eternal law.

  He, the greatest protector of the world, bears them on His own chest,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  12. Dual and Non-Dual

  Everything is dual: two hands, two feet, two ears,

  These two eyes are focused, skilled in instructing the wicked and protecting the
  good.

  Yet He is non-dual, uniquely skilled in protecting the multitude of worlds,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  13. Hand on the Hip

  With His hand on His hip, the Lord displays His grace,

  The great king, the protector of the assembly of citizens and the worlds.

  "I am here, do not fear, I am your refuge," He seems to say with His gaze,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  14. The Varada Hasta (Bestowing Hand)

  The Lord grants boons, discerning the qualities of beings through their births,

  Therefore, one should bow down with reverence and adoration.

  Revealing the means of receiving that, surrender to His feet,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  15. Speculation on the Reason for the Sandalwood Mark

  He wears sandalwood for the sake of the mark, beautiful on Bhrigu''s day,

  Recalling the blow of the foot on His sole, inflicted by Bhrigu in anger.

  Bearing that, He aims for the pacification of His mind and extends grace,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  16. The Message of the Sandalwood Mark

  He who makes three marks here for the world,

  Awakens them to the three qualities, good and bad, arising from the sun.

  He raises His own seed upwards, uniting it with the nectar of yoga,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  17. The Purpose of the Three Marks

  Devotees of Hari make the three marks, for this

  Conceals the space between the eyebrows and controls the gaze.

  Meditation on the form of Hari becomes easy, and one rejoices,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  18. Sandalwood Paste on the Chin

  In ancient times, because of the injury caused by the blow of a cowherd,

  A paste of sandalwood was applied to both sides of His chin with great care.

  Concealing the mark of the wound, He teaches a smiling face, O!

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  19. The Kasturi Tilaka (Musk Mark)

  As the equality of the entire community of beings becomes clear,

  Beasts, birds, insects, are all declared by the Lord, day after day.

  He bears a dark, gentle mark, the source of Brahman,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  20. The Feet

  By obtaining even a bit of the water that washed His feet, the foremost among the
  gods, including the sons of Aditi,

  Desire to serve Him constantly in order to attain their own upliftment.

  Therefore, I serve those feet of the father of Brahma every day,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  21. The Abode of Varaha

  This Varaha, resting on the excellent mountain to the left, desires,

  This ancient form is always worthy of friendship with me.

  Thus, the three worlds have attained a place in His embrace,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  22. The Vision of Varaha

  He dwells in the auspicious lake, cleansing impurities, and shows compassion,

  That Boar, the support of the earth, victorious in protecting the world.

  May Varaha always grant you boons, day and night,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  23. Venkatachala

  The son of the great Meru, the king of mountains, in the Krita Yuga,

  Performed penance to the Lord, to become His abode for the world.

  That peaceful one became Venkatagiri, whose path

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  24. Sheshachala

  When a dispute arose, with Shesha proudly claiming to be the couch of Hari,

  He blew him away with the breath from His mouth.

  "I will dwell on the ancient mountain of Shesha," He declared,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  25. Narayanachala

  He who firmly establishes the wavering mind, which is like water, on the immovable,

  That famous God holds this entire heart.

  Therefore, this mountain is known as Narayanagiri, His dwelling place,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  26. Vrishabhachala

  It is said in the scriptures that Indra, indeed named Vrishabha,

  Always renders service with devotion at Your feet.

  His sacred mountain is Vrishabhagiri, O Lord of sacrifices,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  27. Fame of Vrishadri

  Vrishā, the rain cloud, whose peak is filled with lakes, rivers, and streams,

  Constantly sprinkles them with abundant rain, holding them full of water.

  Thus, the fame of Vrishadri is naturally delightful to behold,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  28. Anjanadri - Because of Hanuman''s Service

  Having placed in her mind the thought of His arrival,

  Anjana Devi performed auspicious service on the mountain.

  To give birth to Hanuman, the famous mountain became known by that name,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.


  29. Anjanadri - Because of Auspiciousness

  Bringing together unparalleled love in the face of marriage,

  Uniting each other constantly, the hearts of the excellent bride and groom.

  Therefore, this peak is well-known as Anjanashikhari, the abode of auspiciousness,

  May Govinda bestow His self-realized, non-dual state upon me.

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  13. కటిహస్తః

  కటౌ హస్తం కృత్వా ప్రభురయి విలాసం ప్రథయతే

  మహారాజో గోప్తా పురజననికాయస్య జగతామ్।

  అహం వర్తే మా భేః శరణమహమిత్యైక్షదహో

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  14. వరదహస్తః

  వరం దత్తే దేవో గుణగుణివివేకేన జనుషాం

  అతః ప్రహ్వీభావీ నమనవిధినైవార్హతి జనః।

  తదాదానోపాయం సమవగమయన్ పాదశరణమ్

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  15. పటీరపుండ్ర హేతూత్ప్రేక్షా

  పటీరం పుండ్రార్థం ధరతి రమణీయం భృగుదినే

  భృగో రోషావిద్ధం చరణతలఘాతం స్మృతిపథే।

  దధానస్స్వం చిత్తప్రశమమభిలక్షయ్య తనుతే

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  16. పటీరపుండ్ర సందేశః

  త్రయం పుండ్రాణాం యో విదధదిహ లోకాయ కురుతే

  ప్రబోధం ప్రోత్థానే తపన గుణ దోష త్రితయతః।

  నిజం రేతశ్చోర్ధ్వం గమయతి సుధాం యోగకలితాం

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  17. త్రిపుండ్ర ప్రయోజనమ్

  త్రిపుండ్రం లక్ష్యస్వం కురుత హరిభక్తా యత ఇదమ్

  హ్నుతే భ్రూమధ్యం వో హరతి పరిదృష్టిం నియమతః

  హరేరూపధ్యానం భవతి సులభం హృష్యతి తతః

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  18. చుబుకే పటీరలేపః

  పురా గోపస్యాభ్రేర్నిహతికృత నైజక్షతిమతః

  పటీరస్యాऽలేపశ్చుబుకయుగలేऽధాయి మహితః

  హ్నువానో బాధాంకం హసితవదనం శిక్షయతి భోః

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  19. కస్తూరీతిలకమ్

  యథా స్పష్టీభూతా సమజననవర్గస్య సమతా

  మృగః పక్షీ కీటః భగవదుదితా ఇత్యనుదినమ్।

  కలం ధత్తే శ్యామం మృదమదమసౌ బ్రహ్మజనకః

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  20. చరణౌ

  యయోస్సేవాలాభాదదితిజముఖా ఉత్తమగణాః

  స్వముత్థానం ప్రాప్తుం పరిచరణమిచ్ఛంతి శతశః।

  తతస్సేవేయాంహం తదజపితృ పాదౌ ప్రతిదినమ్

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  21. వరాహస్య స్థానమ్

  వరాహోऽయం వామం గిరివరమధిష్ఠాయ లషతే

  పురారూపోऽయం మే మయి సఖితయా దర్హతి సదా।

  ఇతి క్రోడస్థానం పరిగతిమియాయ త్రిభువనః

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  22. వరాహ దర్శనమ్

  ప్రశస్తం కాసారం మలహరణమధ్యుష్య దయతే

  స పోత్రీ భూజానిః జగదవనదక్షో విజయతే

  వరాహం నిత్యం వో వరమహని నక్తం చ కురుతాం

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  23. వేంకటాచలమ్

  మహామేరోస్సూనుః ధరణిధరరాజః కృతయుగే

  తపస్తేపే పత్యుః జగత ఉపయాతుం సదనతామ్।

  యయౌ దాంతోऽసౌ వేంకటగిరివరో యస్య పదవీం

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  24. శేషాచలమ్

  వివాదే సఞ్జాతే హరిశయనస్మీతి బహుధా

  పురా దృప్తే శేషే శ్వసనముఖతోऽధ్మాపయదసౌ।

  అహిం జీర్ణం శేషాచలమను నివత్స్యామ్యదయత

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  25. నారాయణాచలమ్

  చలం చిత్తం నారం దృఢమచలమాత్తం విదతి యః

  స విఖ్యాతో దేవో విధరత ఇదం సర్వహృదయమ్।

  తతః ఖ్యాతో నారాయణగిరిరముష్యోదవసితమ్

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  26. వృషభాచల

  శృతౌ ప్రోక్తశ్శక్రః నను వృషభనామా త్వచ్చరణయోః

  సదా భక్త్యావేశాద్ విరచయతి సేవాం పరిచరః।

  తదీయాద్రిశ్శస్తో వృషభగిరిరస్తి క్రతుపతే

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  27. వృషాద్రి ఖ్యాతిః

  వృషా పర్జన్యోऽయం-హ్రదనదనదీర్ యస్య శిఖరే

  సువర్షేణాऽసిఞ్చన్ సలిలపరిపూర్ణాన్ దధదిమామ్।

  వృషాద్రీతిఖ్యాతిం సహజరుచిరాసేచనతామ్

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  28. అఞ్జనాద్రినామా - హనుమత్సేవాయాః

  యదీయాऽగన్తృత్వం మనసి వినిధాయ స్నుషు బుధా-

  ऽఞ్జనా దేవీ చక్రే కుశలవరివస్యాం గిరివరే।

  హనూమన్తం సూతుం ప్రథితగిరిరాసీత్ తదభిధః

  స్వసిద్ధాం గోవిందో ద్వయరహిత సంస్థాం ప్రదిశతామ్।।


  29. అఞ్జనాద్రినామా - కళ్యాణత్వాత్

  అనన్యం ప్రేమాణం పరిణయముఖం యస్య సవిధే

  సమఞ్జన్తోऽన్యోऽన్యం కిల వరవధూహృన్ద్యవిరతమ్।

  అతోऽయం సుఖ్యాతోऽఞ్జనశిఖరికళ్యాణనిలయః
