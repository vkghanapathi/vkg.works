abstract: Govinda Laharī is a devotional lyric poem in Sanskrit composed by Vamshi
  Krishna Ghanapāṭhī in the śikhariṇī meter, celebrating the divine attributes and
  cosmic significance of Lord Vishnu (Govinda). The work comprises philosophical and
  devotional verses structured around traditional themes of Vaishnava theology, each
  stanza concluding with the refrain "svasiddhāṁ govindo dvayrahita saṁsthāṁ pradishtatām"
  (may Govinda, established in non-duality, reveal his own perfection). Composed during
  the author's tenure as an examiner of Vedic students at Tirupati, the work represents
  a synthesis of classical Sanskrit poetic craft with contemporary devotional expression
  within the Śrī Vaishnava tradition.
preamble: This work emerges from the Śrī Vaishnava theological and devotional tradition
  centered on the worship of Vishnu (Govinda) as the supreme reality. The preamble
  situates the composition at Tirupati (Śrī Venkaṭāchala), the principal pilgrimage
  site of this sampradāya, and credits the spiritual guidance of the author's guru,
  Gaṇapati Saccidānanda Svāmī. The verses engage classical themes from Vedānta philosophy—particularly
  the non-dual nature of Brahman, divine compassion (kṛpā), and the accessibility
  of mokṣa through devotion—while employing the ornamental, melodic conventions of
  Sanskrit kāvya to render theological doctrine as lyric meditation.

translation_en: 'Govinda Lahari


  Foreword: By the grace of Lord Srinivasa, this person, appointed as an examiner
  of Vedic students in Sri Venkatachala Kshetra (Tirumala Hills), experienced the
  bliss of residing on the hill during the Navaratri season in the month of Magha.
  It is there, in the presence of the venerable Sri Sri Ganapati Sachchidananda Swamiji,
  my esteemed preceptor, that this poetic composition arose by the will of Sri Pati
  Purushottama, with the intention of describing the cascade of His glories. I humbly
  submit this short poem, composed in the Shikharini meter, dedicated to the description
  of the pinnacle of Sri Pati''s splendor, that it may attain fulfillment through
  the delight of the noble-hearted.


  Addicted to the nectar of Hari''s name –

  Vamshikrishna Ghanapathi


  1.  What indeed shall I do with these words of Hari, which are beyond description?

  Where shall I wander, when this universe is pervaded, and the sky is but a mere
  abode?

  How shall I discern that Supreme Brahman-Atman, which is the basis of my very being?

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  2.  If one, desiring prosperity, strives with diligence,

  His friendship grants me that Lakshmi again and again.

  When the path is the same for us, happiness arises in the company of a friend.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  3.  In ancient times, when the waters were diminished due to the power of the demons,

  He lifted up the Vedas, revealing His true essence.

  To grant the object of contemplation to the intellect, or to sever the cycle of
  birth,

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  4.  He, Sri Nivasa, bears prosperity, possessing immense affection for others,

  And Lakshmi enjoys His affection from Her lotus face.

  He is renowned among the hosts of gods, for bearing the Srivatsa mark.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  5.  Having studied the four Vedas continuously, you are virtuous,

  That one act you have done, may He grant and declare.

  O friend, know that He is the most virtuous, dwelling in the midst of the worlds.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  6.  Paths, dense with various forms of great delusion, dissolve

  In that Supreme Brahman, in that vast, majestic, and eternal abode.

  Wander through the world, and finally attain the supreme abode of the destroyer
  of Madhu.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  7.  If He is dark like a cloud, and a connoisseur of beauty,

  Then why is the dark hue of Krishna so captivating?

  He is dear because of His power of attraction, not because of His color.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  8.  What does this golden chariot accomplish here, constantly awakening the mind?

  These feelings are not easily measured in the infinite and finite.

  There is neither increase nor decrease in the beautiful radiance of His own treasure.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  9.  Abandon dharma and adharma, for He says, "Take refuge in Me."

  Nothing should be done if your mind is fixed that the Lord will protect you.

  Without laziness, worship the Lord, constantly, with a mind dedicated to self-surrender.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  10. This beautiful-browed, slender woman generates joy and curious delight,

  Whether young or affectionate, they are again youthful and fond of pleasure.

  He is the Lord of all beauty; attain Him constantly with your mind.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  11. Lakshmi and Bhudevi are the first to provide life

  To all people, constantly, in an unbroken manner.

  He, the greatest protector of the world, bears them on His chest.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  12. Duality is everywhere: a pair of hands, a pair of feet, and a pair of ears,

  These two eyes are focused, skilled in teaching and protecting the good and punishing
  the wicked.

  Yet, He is non-dual, skilled in protecting the multitude of worlds.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  13. With His hand on His hip, the Lord displays His grace,

  He is the great king, the protector of the assembly of citizens and the worlds.

  "I am here, do not fear; I am your refuge," thus He gazes.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  14. The Lord grants boons, discerning the qualities of beings through their births,

  Therefore, one should bow down with humility, through the act of reverence.

  Revealing the means of receiving that, take refuge in His feet.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  15. He wears sandalwood paste for the sake of the Pundra mark on Bhrigu''s day,

  Remembering the blow of Bhrigu''s foot on His chest.

  Bearing that, He extends His own mind''s peace, aiming for tranquility.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  16. He who creates the three lines of the Pundra mark for the world,

  Awakens understanding of the three qualities and defects of the sun at dawn.

  He raises His own seed upwards, united with the nectar of yoga.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  17. Hari''s devotees wear the three lines of the Pundra mark, for it

  Conceals the space between the eyebrows and regulates the gaze.

  Meditation on Hari''s form becomes easy, and one rejoices.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  18. In ancient times, the cowherd, having suffered an injury,

  Applied sandalwood paste to both cheeks.

  Concealing the mark of injury, He teaches a smiling face.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  19. Just as the equality of all beings is clearly established,

  Be it animal, bird, or insect, as declared by the Lord daily.

  He bears a dark mark, soft and intoxicating, the source of Brahman.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  20. By the touch of whose lotus feet, the hosts of demons and the best of gods

  Desire to serve Him constantly, to attain their own upliftment.

  Therefore, I serve those feet of the father of Brahma daily.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  21. This Varaha, resting on the excellent mountain to the left,

  Is like Shiva to me, and is always worthy of friendship with me.

  Thus, the three worlds have attained a place in His lap.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  22. May He, the auspicious boar, residing in the sacred lake that removes impurities,

  He, the boar-formed, the earth-born, victorious in protecting the world, be gracious.

  May Varaha always grant you boons, day and night.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  23. The son of the great Meru, the king of mountains, in the Krita Yuga,

  Performed penance to the Lord, to become His abode for the world.

  That gentle one became Venkatagiri, whose path is...

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  24. When a dispute arose, with Sesha proudly claiming to be Hari''s bed,

  He blew him away from His mouth.

  "I shall dwell on the ancient mountain of Sesha," thus He showed compassion.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  25. He who firmly establishes the wavering mind as steady and unmoving,

  That famous God holds this entire heart.

  Therefore, this mountain is known as Narayanagiri, His dwelling place.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  26. It is said in the scriptures that Indra is known as Vrishabha, and he

  Always serves Your feet with devotion.

  His mountain is praised as Vrishabhagiri, O Lord of sacrifices.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  27. Vrisa is the rain cloud, whose peak

  Is sprinkled with abundant rain, filling it with water.

  Thus, it is famous as Vrishadri, naturally beautiful and ever-satisfying to behold.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  28. Considering His arrival in her mind, the daughter-in-law,

  Anjana Devi, performed auspicious service on the mountain.

  To give birth to Hanuman, that famous mountain became known by that name.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  29. Bringing together unparalleled love in the face of marriage,

  Constantly uniting the hearts of the excellent bride and groom.

  Therefore, this mountain is well-known as Anjanashikhari, the abode of auspiciousness.

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.


  30. Suparna is Your vehicle...

  May Govinda bestow upon me that state of being, devoid of duality, established in
  Himself.'
