abstract: Govinda Laharī is a devotional lyric poem composed by Vamshi Krishna Ghanapāṭhī
  in the Śikhariṇī metre, celebrating the divine glory and attributes of Lord Viṣṇu
  (Govinda) through a series of interconnected verses. The work was composed in Mārgaśīrṣa
  month during the author's tenure as an examiner of Vedic scholars at the Venkateśvara
  temple on Tirumala hill, under the spiritual guidance of his guru Gaṇapati Saccidānanda
  Swami, and structured as a unified meditation on the deity's non-dual, self-sufficient
  nature.
preamble: Govinda Laharī belongs to the devotional poetic tradition of Vaiṣṇavism,
  specifically the theology and praxis of Śrī Vaiṣṇavism centred on the Venkateśvara
  (Tirupati) temple complex. The work exemplifies the Sanskrit poetic hymn (prabandha)
  as a vehicle for theological exposition, employing the classical Śikhariṇī metre
  and the refrain structure typical of devotional lyric poetry. Each verse addresses
  distinct theological and devotional themes—from the incomprehensibility of the divine
  to the efficacy of surrender—while maintaining formal metrical and thematic unity,
  anchoring them in the salvific principle of God's dual-less (dvaya-rahita) existence.

translation_en: 'Govinda Lahari


  Foreword: By the grace of Lord Srinivasa, this humble person, appointed as an examiner
  of Vedic students in Sri Venkatachala Kshetra (Tirumala Hills), experienced the
  bliss of residing on Adhigiri during the Navaratri season in the month of Magha.
  It is there, in the presence of the esteemed Sri Sri Ganapati Sachchidananda Swamiji,
  my revered Guru, that this poetic composition arose by the will of Sri Pati Purushottama,
  with the intention of describing the cascade of His glories. I humbly submit this
  short poem, composed in the Shikharini meter, dedicated to the description of the
  pinnacle of Sri Pati''s magnificence, hoping it will bring joy to the learned.


  Addicted to the nectar of Hari''s name –

  Vamshikrishna Ghanapathi


  1.  Ineffable,

  What offering can I make of these words at Hari''s feet,

  When the universe is pervaded, and space itself His abode?

  What can I discern of the Supreme Brahman-Atman, my very ground?

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  2.  Pathik Sneha (Friendship of the Traveler)

  If, desiring fortune, one strives with diligence,

  And that friendship grants me Lakshmi again and again,

  When the path is equal for us both, happiness abounds with such a friend.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  3.  Vedoddharana Hetu (Cause of Uplifting the Vedas)

  In ancient times, when the waters were diminished by the demons and the force of
  time,

  He uplifted the Vedas, revealing His own essence.

  To grant the object of contemplation to intellects, or to sever the cycle of birth,

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  4.  Sri Vasah (Abode of Fortune)

  He, Sri Vasa, bears fortune, exceedingly compassionate,

  And fortune cherishes His compassion from Her lotus face.

  He is renowned among the hosts of deities as ''He who wears Srivatsa''.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  5.  Sukrita Hetu (Cause of Merit)

  Having unceasingly studied the four Vedas, you are meritorious,

  That single act you perform, He alone ordains.

  Know this, friend, He is the most meritorious, dwelling in the midst of the world.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  6.  Patham Gamyam (Destination of Paths)

  The paths dissolve, the dense forests of great delusion,

  In this Supreme Brahman, in His vast and glorious essence, the eternal abode.

  Wander through the world, and ultimately rest at the lotus feet of Madhuripu.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  7.  Shyamavarnena na Preyan (Not Beloved by Dark Complexion Alone)

  If He is cloud-dark, a connoisseur of beauty,

  Then how can the dark complexion alone be the most enchanting?

  He is beloved due to His attractive nature, not merely His color.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  8.  Suvarna Vimanam (Golden Chariot)

  What does this golden chariot accomplish here, constantly awakening?

  These sentiments are not easily measured in the infinite and finite.

  There is neither increase nor decrease in the beautiful radiance of this treasure
  of the Self.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  9.  Sarva Dharma Parityagah (Abandonment of All Dharmas)

  Abandon dharma and adharma, for He says, ''Take refuge in Me''.

  Nothing need be done if your mind is fixed on the Lord protecting you.

  Without laziness, worship the Lord, constantly, with a mind surrendered to the Self.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  10. Saundarya Mohah (Delusion of Beauty)

  This beautiful-browed, slender woman generates joy and delightful infatuation,

  Or a youthful, affectionate man, or those who are fond of youthful pleasures.

  He is the Lord of all beauty – attain Him constantly with your mind.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  11. Sridevi Bhudevyoh Vakshasthala Nivasah (Residence on the Bosom of Sridevi and
  Bhudevi)

  May Mahalakshmi and Bhudevi be the first to grant life,

  To all people, He causes them to traverse by an unceasing method.

  He, the greatest protector of the world, holds them on His own chest.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  12. Dvayam Advayam (Duality, Non-Duality)

  All is dual: two hands, two feet, two ears,

  These two eyes are focused, skilled in disciplining the wicked and protecting the
  good.

  Yet, He is non-dual, uniquely skilled in protecting the multitude of worlds.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  13. Katihastah (Hand on the Hip)

  The Lord, with His hand on His hip, displays His grace,

  The great king, the protector of the assembly of citizens, of the worlds.

  ''I am here, do not fear, I am your refuge,'' thus He gazes.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  14. Varadahastah (Bestowing Hand)

  The Lord bestows boons, discerning the qualities and merits of beings,

  Therefore, one should bow down with reverence and humility.

  Revealing the means of receiving that boon, He offers refuge at His feet.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  15. Patirapundra Hetutpreksha (Speculation on the Reason for the Sandalwood Mark)

  He wears sandalwood paste for the mark, beautifully, on Bhrigu''s day,

  Remembering the blow of Bhrigu''s foot on His sole in anger.

  Bearing that, He aims for the pacification of His own mind and extends grace.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  16. Patirapundra Sandeshah (Message of the Sandalwood Mark)

  He who applies the three marks for the sake of the world,

  Arouses awareness at the time of rising, from the three qualities and defects of
  the sun.

  He raises His own seed upwards, combined with the nectar of yoga.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  17. Tripundra Prayojanam (Purpose of the Three Marks)

  Devotees of Hari apply the three marks, for it

  Conceals the space between the eyebrows, and regularly removes distractions.

  Meditation on the form of Hari becomes easy, and one rejoices.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  18. Chubuke Patiralepah (Sandalwood Paste on the Chin)

  In ancient times, due to an injury caused by a cowherd,

  The application of sandalwood paste was applied to both sides of His chin.

  Concealing the mark of the blow, He teaches a smiling face.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  19. Kasturitilakam (Musk Tilaka)

  Just as the equality of the entire class of beings becomes clear,

  Beast, bird, insect, are all spoken of by the Lord, daily.

  He bears a dark, beautiful mark, the gentle fragrance, the source of Brahman.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  20. Charanau (Feet)

  By the slightest touch of which, the hosts of demons and the best of beings

  Desire to serve and attain their upliftment hundreds of times.

  Therefore, I serve those feet of the father of Brahma, daily.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  21. Varahasya Sthanam (Abode of Varaha)

  This Varaha delights, residing on the excellent mountain,

  This ancient form is always worthy of friendship with me.

  Thus, the three worlds have attained a place in His embrace.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  22. Varaha Darshanam (Vision of Varaha)

  May He, dwelling in the excellent lake that removes impurities, be compassionate,

  May that Boar, the support of the earth, skilled in protecting the world, be victorious.

  May Varaha grant you boons, daily, day and night.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  23. Venkatachalam

  The son of Mahameru, the king of mountains, in the Krita Yuga,

  Performed penance to the Lord, to become His abode for the world.

  That subdued one became Venkatagiri, whose path leads to

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  24. Seshachalam

  When a dispute arose, with Sesha proudly claiming to be the couch of Hari,

  He blew him away with the breath from His mouth.

  ''I shall dwell on this ancient, worn-out Seshachala,'' He declared.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  25. Narayanachalam

  He who firmly establishes the wavering mind, the essence, on the unmoving,

  That famous Lord, He holds this entire heart.

  Therefore, this mountain is known as Narayanagiri, His dwelling.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  26. Vrishabhachalah

  It is said in the scriptures that Indra is indeed named Vrishabha, and at Your feet

  He always renders service with devotion and absorption.

  His sacred mountain is Vrishabhagiri, O Lord of sacrifices.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  27. Vrishadri Khyatih (Fame of Vrishadri)

  Vrisa is the rain cloud, whose peak

  Is sprinkled with abundant rain, filling them with water.

  Thus, the fame of Vrishadri is naturally delightful and ever-satisfying to behold.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  28. Anjanadri Nama - Hanumatsevayah (Name of Anjanadri - Due to Hanuman''s Service)

  Having placed in her mind the thought of His arrival,

  Anjana Devi performed auspicious service on the mountain.

  To give birth to Hanuman, that famous mountain became known by that name.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  29. Anjanadri Nama - Kalyanatvat (Name of Anjanadri - Due to Auspiciousness)

  Bringing together unparalleled love in the face of marriage,

  Constantly uniting bride and groom, heart to heart.

  Therefore, this mountain is well-known as the auspicious abode of Anjana.

  May Govinda grant that self-realized state, devoid of duality.


  30. Garudadri

  Your Garuda is the vehicle, dispelling the fear of snakes, with excellent fame,

  Serving the Lord.'

  2. పథిక స్నేహః

  సంపదను కోరుకుంటే, శ్రద్ధతో ప్రయత్నిస్తే,

  గోవిందుడు ద్వైతరహితమైన స్వీయ సిద్ధిని ప్రసాదించుగాక!

  గోవిందుడు ద్వైతరహితమైన స్వీయ సిద్ధిని ప్రసాదించుగాక!


  4. శ్రీ వాసః

  శ్రీవత్సాంకితుడని దేవతలచే కొనియాడబడినవాడు.

  గోవిందుడు ద్వైతరహితమైన స్వీయ సిద్ధిని ప్రసాదించుగాక!


  5. సుకృత హేతుః

  నాలుగు వేదాలను నిరంతరం అధ్యయనం చేయడం వలన నీవు పుణ్యాత్ముడవు,

  గోవిందుడు ద్వైతరహితమైన స్వీయ సిద్ధిని ప్రసాదించుగాక!


  6. పథాం గమ్యమ్

  గోవిందుడు ద్వైతరహితమైన స్వీయ సిద్ధిని ప్రసాదించుగాక!


  7. శ్యామవర్ణేన న ప్రేయాన్

  8. సువర్ణ విమానమ్

  ఈ బంగారు విమానం ఇక్కడ ఏమి చేస్తుంది, నిరంతరం మేల్కొలుపుతూ?

  గోవిందుడు ద్వైతరహితమైన స్వీయ సిద్ధిని ప్రసాదించుగాక!

  గోవిందుడు ద్వైతరహితమైన స్వీయ సిద్ధిని ప్రసాదించుగాక!


  10. సౌందర్య మోహః
