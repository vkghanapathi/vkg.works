abstract: A devotional and protective composition attributed to Yājñavalkya that presents
  the Gāyatrī Kavaca (Armour of Gāyatrī), a mantra-based protective formula mapping
  the syllables of the sacred Gāyatrī mantra to different parts and directions of
  the body, followed by an eight-verse hymn of praise to Gāyatrī (Gāyatrī Aṣṭakam).
  The work exemplifies the Tantric integration of Vedic mantra with body-centred ritual
  protection and feminine divine worship.
preamble: This composition stands within the Vedic devotional and Tantric traditions,
  drawing on the Gāyatrī mantra—the most revered mantra of the Rigveda and central
  to Vedic ritual and meditation practices. The Kavaca (protective armour) genre appears
  throughout Hindu ritual literature as a method of invoking divine protection through
  the sacred geometry and syllabic power (śakti) of mantras. The attribution to Yājñavalkya,
  the celebrated Vedic sage and author of the Yājñavalkya Smṛti, lends scriptural
  authority. The accompanying Aṣṭakam embeds Gāyatrī within the broader feminine divine
  principle (Śakti), addressing her through multiple goddess forms—Sāvitrī, Sarasvatī,
  Vaiṣṇavī—reflecting post-Vedic theological developments that identify the abstract
  mantra with personal, accessible forms of the Goddess.

translation_en: "Thus spoke Yājñavalkya:\n\n1.  Hearken, O tiger among ascetics, to\
  \ the armor that increases merit, divine, sacred to Gāyatrī, and the destroyer of\
  \ all sins.\n\n    *Viniyogaḥ*: ॐ Of this Gāyatrī Kavacha, Brahmā is the Ṛṣi, Gāyatrī\
  \ is the Chandaḥ (meter), Gāyatrī is the Devatā (deity). Its application is for\
  \ the attainment of Dharma, Artha, Kāma, and Moksha.\n\n2.  May Gāyatrī protect\
  \ me from the East, and Sāvitrī protect me from the South. May Brahmasandhyā protect\
  \ me from the West, and Vaishnavi protect me from the North.\n\n3.  May Pāvakī,\
  \ who is Sarasvatī, protect my direction of fire (Southeast). May Brahmaṇī, of the\
  \ form of Brahman, protect my upward direction.\n\n4.  May Vaishnavi protect me\
  \ from below; thus, may she protect me from all directions. May Gāyatrī protect\
  \ my head, and Sāvitrī protect my forehead.\n\n5.  May Sarasvatī protect my ears,\
  \ and Śaṅkarī protect the corners of my eyes. May Sarasvatī, devoted to Brahman,\
  \ protect my nose.\n\n6.  May Viśvāmitra Ṛṣi always protect my tongue in the East.\
  \ May Gāyatrī protect my mouth, and Sarasvatī protect my teeth.\n\n7.  May Vaishnavi\
  \ protect my throat with the single-syllable mantra 'ॐ'. May Sāvitrī protect my\
  \ neck, and the goddess Sarasvatī protect my back.\n\n8.  May Maheśvarī, in the\
  \ form of the syllable 'Tat', protect my hands. May Sāvitrī, of the nature of Praṇava,\
  \ protect my thighs, denoted by 'Savitur'.\n\n9.  May the deity protect my navel\
  \ region, denoted by 'Vareṇyam', and protect my waist. May 'Bhargo' protect my loins,\
  \ and Pārvatī protect my secret parts.\n\n10. May 'Devasya' protect my knees, and\
  \ 'Dhīmahi' protect my shanks. May 'Dhiyaḥ' protect my feet, and 'Yaḥ' protect my\
  \ toes.\n\n11. May 'Naḥ', in the form of a word, protect my ankles, and 'Prachodayāt'\
  \ protect the end of my feet. This divine armor destroys hundreds of obstacles.\n\
  \nGayatri Ashtakam\n\n1.  I adore Ambā Gāyatrī, the source of supreme auspiciousness\
  \ and bliss, who is of auspicious speech, whose feet are worshipped by the best\
  \ of gods and sages, who is Śivā, the primordial, the venerable, consisting of the\
  \ three worlds, the mother of the Vedas, the supreme power, the creator of diverse\
  \ forms, and full of qualities.\n\n2.  I adore Ambā Gāyatrī, the source of supreme\
  \ auspiciousness and bliss, who is pure, established in Sattva, the remover of all\
  \ kinds of adverse conditions, formless, the essence, of supremely pure ascetic\
  \ form, incomparable, the eldest and best of the world, worshipped by gods and demons,\
  \ and praised by the scriptures.\n\n3.  I adore Ambā Gāyatrī, the source of supreme\
  \ auspiciousness and bliss, who is devoted to asceticism, the fulfiller of desires,\
  \ the pacifier of the mental anguish of her own people, the embodiment of compassion,\
  \ the inspiration, easily accessible to the assembly of ascetics through grace,\
  \ the venerable, the sacred, and the remover of all worldly bonds.\n\n4.  I adore\
  \ Ambā Gāyatrī, the source of supreme auspiciousness and bliss, who is always worthy\
  \ of worship, attainable, the expander of good intellect and wisdom, the dispeller\
  \ of sorrow and darkness, the remover of the darkness of delusion residing in the\
  \ heart, the supreme, the divine, the auspicious, and the sole raft for crossing\
  \ the ocean of worldly existence.\n\n5.  I adore Ambā Gāyatrī, the source of supreme\
  \ auspiciousness and bliss, who is unborn, dual, threefold, of diverse qualities\
  \ and forms, supremely pure, the destroyer of darkness, the string (of the Vedas),\
  \ of sweet sound to the ears, full of rasa (essence), highly venerable, blessed,\
  \ always compassionate, and of glorious power.\n\n6.  I adore Ambā Gāyatrī, the\
  \ source of supreme auspiciousness and bliss, who is the sustainer of the world,\
  \ the vessel, the cause of the destruction of all worldly existence, supremely valiant,\
  \ steadfast, the path of a supremely pure mass of asceticism, the one without a\
  \ second, the seat of the entire universe.\n\n7.  I adore Ambā Gāyatrī, the source\
  \ of supreme auspiciousness and bliss, who is awakened, enlightened, the remover\
  \ of the dullness of her own people, golden, virtuous, sung by good poets, supremely\
  \ skilled, the supreme knowledge, the primordial, the possessor of pure qualities,\
  \ and the divine.\n\n8.  I adore Ambā Gāyatrī, the source of supreme auspiciousness\
  \ and bliss, who is infinite, peaceful, worshipped by the assembly of the wise,\
  \ consisting of the scriptures, easy to sing, worthy of meditation, whom the lord\
  \ of the gods remembers in his heart always, always affectionate due to devotion,\
  \ power, and humility.\n\nPhalaśruti (Fruit of Listening):\n\nWhoever recites this\
  \ auspicious Gāyatrī Aṣṭakam with a pure heart will always have great fortune, and\
  \ the Mother will be pleased with him.\n\nThus ends the Gāyatrī Aṣṭakam.\n\nToday\
  \ is Deepavali, auspicious and illuminating the light of knowledge.\nMay it bring\
  \ an abundance of joy to your home."
