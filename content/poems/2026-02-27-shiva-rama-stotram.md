abstract: Deva Dvādaśī Stotram is a twelve-fold hymnic composition dedicated to Lord
  Śiva, structured as a liturgical prayer incorporating invocation, sacred geography,
  worship procedures, mythological narrative, and benedictory conclusions. The work
  synthesizes Vedic ritual language (Rudra Yāga terminology), devotional Sanskrit
  poetics, and Tantric theological concepts, presenting Śiva in his multivalent aspects
  as creator, destroyer, benefactor, and transcendent reality.
preamble: This composition belongs to the Śaiva devotional and liturgical tradition,
  particularly drawing on the Rudrayāmala and Śaiva Āgamic frameworks. The text is
  organized according to classical stotram structure (invocation, territorial/sacred
  site specification, worship performance, mythic meditation, and results of recitation),
  reflecting post-Vedic Hindu ritual systematization. The dual-language presentation
  in Sanskrit and Telugu indicates its usage within South Indian temple contexts,
  likely associated with the worship of Śiva during specific ritual calendars (dvādaśī—twelfth
  lunar day observances). The work demonstrates the integration of Vedic mantra science
  (Om Namah Śivāya), epic narrative motifs (Tripurāntaka—destruction of the three
  cities), and bhakti sensibility.

translation_en: "1. O Lord, God of Gods, Śaṅkara, bestower of fearlessness, have mercy!\
  \ O Supreme Lord, salutations to Your feet, radiant with the name of Rāma. May my\
  \ mind find dissolution in Your abode, in the great dissolution, granting me the\
  \ eight siddhis, famed in the world, fulfilling cherished desires.\n   Om Namaḥ\
  \ Śivāya... Om Namaḥ Śivāya... Om Namaḥ Śivāya Dīkṣiteśvara...\n   Sacred Places:\
  \ Ayodhyā – Kāśī / Śrīśailam\n\n2. Whose divine form is bathed by the multitude\
  \ of waters from the great river (Gaṅgā), Who has embraced divinity for the delight\
  \ of the eternally perfect Nanda, Whose beautiful darśana draws millions of devotees,\
  \ Whose wide eyes drink in the auspicious vision of divinity.\n   Om Namaḥ Śivāya\
  \ Hara, Om Namaḥ Śivāya.\n\n3. May Your beauty be worshipped with a thousand salutations,\
  \ O Beautiful Lord! Again and again, through Rudra Yāga and Homa rituals, O Lord,\
  \ may purity be spread through great abhiṣeka. May You, Lord of Umā, delight through\
  \ the grace of Your gaze.\n\n4. The destroyer at the time of dissolution, the creator,\
  \ fierce with great arrows in battle, ever victorious in every conflict, Who causes\
  \ the dissolution of all living beings at the time of great destruction, and Who,\
  \ to cleanse the remaining karmic residue of beings, again manifests as the pure\
  \ creator, the all-pervading Lord, I worship.\n\n5. Worshipped by names that are\
  \ pleasing to the mind and grant cherished desires, adorned with the sole fortune\
  \ of destroying mental distractions, the giver of the great boon that is the means\
  \ of liberation from mental afflictions, the bestower of desired accomplishments\
  \ through words of salutation, the unparalleled Lord, I bow to You.\n\n6. You reduced\
  \ the three cities to ashes in an instant, O Lord, with Agni as the arrow's point,\
  \ Soma as the shaft, and Viṣṇu as the energy. I worship You here, the primeval Puruṣa,\
  \ holding the great arrow and bow, the destroyer of the three cities.\n\n7. Holding\
  \ the three arrows that protect the world in the form of food, wind, and rain, You\
  \ are intent on the sustenance of the worlds. Supremely skilled in nourishing all\
  \ living beings, O Great Lord, You dispel terrible distress and hunger, distributing\
  \ sustenance.\n\n8. The Lord whose path is free from desire, yet who showers down\
  \ desires at will, who burned Manmatha to ashes with the fire of His forehead eye\
  \ with ease, the destroyer of inauspicious causes and sorrow, the perfection of\
  \ bestowing cherished gifts, fulfilling endless desires without fear, I praise.\n\
  \n9. Who delights in consuming the minds of those who waver, Who always pleases\
  \ the intellect with His steady nature and unwavering grace, Who nourishes the class\
  \ of wise men, bestowing especially pure consciousness, may that Mallikārjuna Śiva\
  \ grant bliss.\n   (May Sadāśiva always be happy.)\n   (Steadiness in the sky)\n\
  \n10. Umā's consort, the primeval one, praised as the father of the three worlds,\
  \ the giver of sustenance for the life of all beings, the father who protects by\
  \ giving clothing, shelter, and other gifts, constantly revealing, \"I am always\
  \ here,\" I praise Him.\n\n11. I become, I contemplate, the auspicious, the self-characteristic\
  \ of the earth. In fear, no one raises their arms in challenge. Actions cease, things\
  \ fall apart, delayed. Therefore, may Śiva, awakening people from fear, through\
  \ intellect and knowledge, protect us.\n\n12. May Dhūrjaṭi, adorned with the Rudra\
  \ Yāga prescribed by the Śrutis, be pleased by the sound of the Rudra mantra and\
  \ the chanting of His names. May the unborn Lord, the ocean of compassion, Śiva,\
  \ be propitiated, and may He, always worshipped by the gods, shine brightly in the\
  \ heart with reverence.\n\n13. Those who desire to experience the nature of existence,\
  \ consciousness, and bliss, study this hymn, which is the essence of Rāma's name\
  \ and the deity of the Vedas, Rudra."
