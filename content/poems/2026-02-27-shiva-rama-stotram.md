  worship procedures, mythological narrative, and benedictory conclusions. The work

  poetics, and Tantric theological concepts, presenting Śiva in his multivalent aspects
  as creator, destroyer, benefactor, and transcendent reality.

  (Om Namah Śivāya), epic narrative motifs (Tripurāntaka—destruction of the three
  cities), and bhakti sensibility.

  \ O Supreme Lord, obeisance to Your feet, radiant with the name of Rāma. May my\
  \ mind find dissolution in Your abode, in the great dissolution, granting me the\
  \ eight siddhis, famed in the world, fulfilling cherished desires.\n   Om Namaḥ\
  \ Śivāya... Om Namaḥ Śivāya... Om Namaḥ Śivāya Dīkṣiteśvara...\n   Holy Places:\
  \ Ayodhyā – Kāśī / Śrīśailam\n\n2. Whose divine form is bathed by the multitude\
  \ of waters from the great river (Gaṅgā), Who embodies perfection, Whose divinity\
  \ is embraced for the joy of Nanda.\n   Whose beautiful darśana attracts millions\
  \ of devotees, Whose wide eyes drink in the auspicious and divine vision.\n   Om\
  \ Namaḥ Śivāya Hara, Om Namaḥ Śivāya.\n\n3. May thousands of prostrations be offered\
  \ to You, O Beautiful Lord. Again and again, through Rudra Yāga and Homa rituals,\
  \ O Lord, May purity be expanded through great abhiṣeka.\n   May the Lord of Umā\
  \ delight, through the grace of Your gaze.\n\n4. The destroyer during pralaya, and\
  \ the creator, Fierce with great arrows, victorious in every battle, At the time\
  \ of great destruction, the one who dissolves the collection of all living beings.\n\
  \   For the sake of cleansing the residue of past karma of living beings, I worship\
  \ the all-pervading Lord, who recreates the spotless universe.\n\n5. Worshipped\
  \ by names that are pleasing to the mind and fulfill desires, Adorned with the unique\
  \ fortune of destroying mental distractions, The bestower of great boons that lead\
  \ to the cessation of mental afflictions,\n   The independent Lord, who grants desired\
  \ accomplishments through words of reverence.\n\n6. You reduced the three cities\
  \ to ashes in an instant, O Lord, with Agni as Your foot, Soma as the arrow's point,\
  \ and Viṣṇu as its energy. I worship You here, the primeval Puruṣa, holding the\
  \ great arrow and bow,\n   You who annihilated the three cities.\n\n7. Holding the\
  \ three arrows that protect the world in the form of food, wind, and rain, Sustaining\
  \ the worlds with compassion, The great Lord, supremely skilled in nourishing all\
  \ living beings,\n   Dispelling terrible distress and hunger, distributing sustenance.\n\
  \n8. The Lord who effortlessly controls desires, yet showers down desired objects,\
  \ Who burned Manmatha with the fire of His forehead eye with ease, The destroyer\
  \ of inauspicious causes and sorrow, the perfection of granting desired boons,\n\
  \   Dispelling fear, fulfilling endless desires without trepidation.\n\n9. Feeding\
  \ on the minds that wander restlessly with His own seat, Always pleasing the intellect\
  \ with steadfast nature and abundance, Nourishing the assembly of wise men, bestowing\
  \ especially pure consciousness,\n   May that Mallikārjuna Śiva delight and dissolve\
  \ (us in Himself).\n   (May Sadāśiva always be happy)\n   (Firm in His own abode)\n\
  \n10. Umā's consort, the primeval one, praised as the father of the three worlds,\
  \ The giver of sustenance for the life of all beings, The father who protects by\
  \ giving clothes, wealth, and shelter,\n   \"I am always here,\" thus constantly\
  \ revealing Himself, I praise Him.\n\n11. I become, I contemplate, the auspicious,\
  \ the self-characteristic of the earth, In fear, no one raises their arms even slightly.\
  \ When things are delayed, they fall apart, therefore, may Śiva, awakening people\
  \ with knowledge and wisdom through fear, protect us.\n\n12. May Dhūrjati, adorned\
  \ with the Rudra Yāga ordained by the Śrutis, be pleased, May He be in harmony with\
  \ the chanting of Rudra mantras and names. May the unborn Lord, the ocean of compassion,\
  \ the auspicious Śiva, be pleased,\n   May He always shine in the heart, worshipped\
  \ by the gods with great reverence.\n\n13. Those people who desire to experience\
  \ the form of existence, consciousness, and bliss, Recite this hymn, which is the\
  \ essence of the Rāma-nāma and dedicated to the deity Rudra."

  చేయబడింది.'
