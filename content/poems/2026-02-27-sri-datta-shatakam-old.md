abstract: The Śrī Datta Śatakam is a devotional hymnic composition of one hundred
  Sanskrit verses (ślokas) dedicated to Lord Dattātreya, composed by Vamshi Krishna
  Ghanapāṭhī and consecrated during the Devī Śaraṇ Navratri festival and Sarasvatī
  Pūjā observance on 13 October 2010. The work systematically expounds the metaphysical
  nature, divine incarnation, genealogy, nomenclature, supremacy, Vedic association,
  trinitarian symbolism, transcendental attributes, and ascetic conduct of Dattātreya
  through the traditional verse framework of the Śataka (century) form.
preamble: This composition belongs to the Dattātreya bhakti tradition within Advaita
  Vedānta practice, venerating Dattātreya as the unified manifestation of Brahmā,
  Viṣṇu, and Śiva. Dattātreya is recognized in Hindu scriptural tradition as a paradigmatic
  guru-figure and embodied teaching principle (avatāra) whose doctrine synthesizes
  Vedic knowledge and renunciation. The work situates itself within the commemoration
  of Dattātreya Jayantī—the birth anniversary of the deity—and invokes the patronage
  of contemporary realized masters (Śrī Gaṇapati Saccidānanda Svāmī and other avadhūta
  gurus) whose lineage preserves this philosophical and spiritual transmission.

translation_en: "Homage to the Most Revered Avadhuta Datta Pithadheeshwara\nShri Ganapati\
  \ Sachchidananda Swamiji, by whose Divine Blessings\nThis Shri Datta Shatakam, composed\
  \ by Vamshikrishna Ghanapathi,\nis offered at the Holy Presence\nVikruti Samvatsara\
  \ Devi Sharannavaratra Mahotsava\n13 – 10 -2010 Saraswati Puja\n(Dedicated during\
  \ the Vikruti Samvat Dattatreya Jayanti Mahotsava)\n\nSubmission:\nMay my revered\
  \ Guru, the great sacrificer, source of speech,\nilluminate my dull intellect with\
  \ the radiant light of wisdom,\nrevealing the nature of Dattatreya, whose essence\
  \ shines forth.\nI adore that Ganapati Yamini, Sachchidananda Datta, the embodiment\
  \ of Brahman.\n\nMay this garland of a hundred gem-like verses, imbued with the\
  \ power of mantras\nobtained through long service to the Guru, adorn the lotus feet\
  \ of Shri Guru.\nVamshi Krishna, seeking the attainment of profound knowledge\n\
  through the grace of the Self, offers this with joy.\n\n(Pṛṇa – to please), Vidha\
  \ – to perform\n\nThe Purpose of Incarnation:\n1.  I adore that Sachchidananda Datta,\
  \ ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who incarnated on earth, the unparalleled\
  \ Lord, to uplift all beings,\n    who manifested to proclaim the glory of the Guru's\
  \ feet,\n    and who, as the Atman of Brahma, grants liberation from the cycle of\
  \ birth and death in the three worlds.\n\nThe Birth:\n2.  I adore that Sachchidananda\
  \ Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who, desired by Anasuya and Atri,\
  \ renowned for their unparalleled penance,\n    manifested on Rikshadri to bestow\
  \ grace upon the world,\n    born as the son of Atri on the full moon of Margashirsha,\
  \ for the establishment of auspiciousness in the world.\n\nThe Glory of the Name:\n\
  3.  I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who\
  \ accepted the name Dattatreya, supremely established and skillful\n    in benefiting\
  \ the world, ever adept and powerful in protecting devotees at all times,\n    a\
  \ name that is pleasing, delightful, and purifying to those who chant it with devotion.\n\
  \n4.  I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    the\
  \ mere remembrance of whose abundant name reduces heaps of sins to ashes,\n    whose\
  \ most beloved prayer generates supreme and unprecedented merit,\n    whose profound\
  \ awakening, when contemplated, carries one across the vast ocean of worldly existence.\n\
  \nThe Greatness:\n5.  I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my\
  \ heart,\n    who is greater than the creators, bestowing knowledge and detachment\
  \ upon those who take refuge in Him,\n    who is greater than the controllers, granting\
  \ countless riches to those who bow down to Him,\n    who is greater than the givers,\
  \ for He is the cause of destroying the desires of the mind.\n\nThe Association\
  \ with Dogs Representing the Vedas:\n6.  I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever\
  \ compassionate in my heart,\n    who, as the embodiment of consciousness, eternal\
  \ truth, the foundation of the world, and the unparalleled reality,\n    the one\
  \ Sat, the supreme Brahman manifesting as countless Jivas, the all-pervading essence,\n\
  \    reveals this principle and delights in the company of dogs representing the\
  \ Vedas.\n\nThe Characteristics of the Trinity:\n7.  I adore that Sachchidananda\
  \ Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who, holding a conch and a discus\
  \ in two hands, reveals the nature of Hari, the preserver,\n    bearing the trident\
  \ and the damaru in the middle hands, signifying the nature of Shambhu, the destroyer,\n\
  \    and holding the rosary, water pot, and Vedas, representing the nature of Brahma,\
  \ the creator.\n\nThe Six Signs – Destroyers of Emotional Distortions:\n8.  I adore\
  \ that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who, perceiving\
  \ the six emotions that arise in the world of births due to illusion,\n    which\
  \ are effects of Maya, causing the delusion of reality through experience,\n   \
  \ removes those distortions, being Himself immutable, by observing them with His\
  \ six signs.\n\nThe Bestowal of Gifts:\n9.  I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever\
  \ compassionate in my heart,\n    whose two hands are insufficient to give if He\
  \ intends to bestow,\n    granting liberation from bondage in the case of the desireless,\
  \ and fulfilling all desires in the case of the desirous.\n    What can I say? He,\
  \ overflowing with compassion, bestows the great bliss of Sachchidananda.\n\nThe\
  \ State of Avadhuta:\n10. I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate\
  \ in my heart,\n    who initiated Himself into the state of Avadhuta, renouncing\
  \ the attachments to honor, possessions, wife, children, home, heaven, fame, etc.,\n\
  \    for the sake of knowledge and detachment for countless devotees,\n    revealing\
  \ the essence of the Upanishads, the creeper of Vedic wisdom.\n\nBeing the Guru\
  \ of the World (24 Gurus):\n11. I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate\
  \ in my heart,\n    who, by attributing the signs of knowledge to twenty-four entities,\n\
  \    established Himself as the Guru of the world, illuminating the path,\n    revealing\
  \ the greatness of being hailed as the Guru of the Universe.\n\nThe Instruction\
  \ to King Yadu:\n12. I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my\
  \ heart,\n    who, seeing all beings burning and suffering in the fire of worldly\
  \ existence,\n    appeared to King Yadu, like an elephant immersed in the Ganges,\n\
  \    and imparted the essence of unlimited joy, being the ocean of wisdom.\n\nThe\
  \ Nature of Acceptance and Rejection:\n13. I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever\
  \ compassionate in my heart,\n    who, understanding that the multitude of qualities\
  \ in all objects in the three worlds\n    teaches its own unique lesson, constantly\
  \ and joyfully,\n    scrutinizing with subtle insight, experiencing bliss, and realizing\
  \ \"I am not enlightened.\"\n\nThe Earth:\n14. I adore that Sachchidananda Datta,\
  \ ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who, saying that the Earth is the first and\
  \ foremost Guru, bearing all things patiently,\n    remaining steadfast even when\
  \ trampled upon, enduring and giving all for the sake of others,\n    taught the\
  \ essence of selfless service and endurance.\n\nThe Air:\n15. I adore that Sachchidananda\
  \ Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who, saying that the Air is my Guru,\
  \ benefiting the entire world through its association with all objects,\n    carrying\
  \ the fragrance but remaining unaffected, conveying both the good and bad qualities\
  \ of things,\n    taught the essence of detachment.\n\nThe Space:\n16. I adore that\
  \ Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who, being all-pervading\
  \ like space, appears to be divided by pots, monasteries, and bodies,\n    but is\
  \ in reality vast, immeasurable, without beginning or end,\n    and who, being the\
  \ embodiment of the Atman, declared, \"Thus I have realized the infinity of the\
  \ Self.\"\n\nThe Water:\n17. I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate\
  \ in my heart,\n    who, saying that the waters are my compassionate Gurus, shining\
  \ brightly, pure in essence, and desirable,\n    being colorless, gentle, devoid\
  \ of specific tastes, and the essence of rasa,\n    taught that one should attain\
  \ purity like them and become a sage.\n\nThe Fire:\n18. I adore that Sachchidananda\
  \ Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who, saying that the fire with its\
  \ seven flames, consuming all, accepts even my Guru's position,\n    burns away\
  \ all impurities and destroys all faults through its brilliance,\n    taught that\
  \ I have thus attained the nature of fire.\n\nThe Moon:\n19. I adore that Sachchidananda\
  \ Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who, saying that the moon has accepted\
  \ this position of my Guru, with its waxing and waning,\n    the body bears the\
  \ birth and death of all beings, and is ever proceeding towards death,\n    taught\
  \ that the visible is perishable, but the Atman is eternal.\n\nThe Sun:\n20. I adore\
  \ that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who, saying that\
  \ the sun is the teacher, visible during the day with its diverse rays,\n    appearing\
  \ differently in pots, rivers, lakes, and waterfalls, though being one and the same,\n\
  \    taught that the Atman resides in various beings in diverse ways.\n\nThe Bird:\n\
  21. I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who,\
  \ saying that the bird is my teacher, whose sweet sounds ceased when it died due\
  \ to the hunter's arrow,\n    seeing its wife caught in the net, along with its\
  \ young ones, afflicted with sorrow,\n    taught that attachment leads to sorrow,\
  \ and one should awaken to discrimination.\n\nThe Ocean:\n22. I adore that Sachchidananda\
  \ Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who, saying that the ocean contains\
  \ countless gems and immeasurable treasures in its deep depths,\n    receiving the\
  \ flow of large and small rivers, yet remaining calm and unified,\n    taught that\
  \ I have learned the qualities of depth and tranquility from the ocean.\n\nThe Python:\n\
  23. I adore that Sachchidananda Datta, ever compassionate in my heart,\n    who,\
  \ saying that the python is content with whatever comes its way, be it little or\
  \ much,\n    lives happily, remaining steadfast in its vow, with whatever food it\
  \ obtains."
