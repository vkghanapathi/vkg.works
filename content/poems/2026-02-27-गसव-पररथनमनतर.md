abstract: A devotional prayer-hymn sequence comprising eight verses dedicated to cow
  veneration (gosevā), composed in Sanskrit. Each verse invokes the cow as Kāmadhenu
  (the wish-fulfilling divine cow) and a bestower of blessings, presenting her multifaceted
  roles in Vedic sacrifice, sustenance, and spiritual welfare, concluding with a repeated
  refrain requesting divine grace and protection.
preamble: This work belongs to the Hindu devotional literature tradition emphasizing
  the sanctity of cows in Vedic cosmology and ritual practice. The cow holds a central
  position in Indian dharma as both a material provider of nourishment and a conduit
  for spiritual merit. This sequence of prayers draws on classical Sanskrit conventions
  of stotras and prārthanās (praises and supplications), invoking Kāmadhenu—the mythological
  celestial cow—as an intermediary to secure material prosperity, ancestral satisfaction,
  and moral protection. The work reflects a synthesis of Vedic ritualism (evident
  in references to agnihotra and abhiṣeka) with devotional practice (bhakti) addressed
  to the living cow as a manifestation of the divine.

translation_en: '1.  She is the Mother of the Vasus, the Daughter of Rudra, the Sister
  of the Adityas, so it is heard. May that Kamadhenu, bestower of boons, tranquil,
  partake of this offering presented by me.


  2.  Cows are mothers, givers of milk, wandering for the good of the world. May that
  Kamadhenu, bestower of boons, tranquil, partake of this offering presented by me.


  3.  She who is milked morning and evening, and protects the calves and mankind.
  May that Kamadhenu, bestower of boons, tranquil, partake of this offering presented
  by me.


  4.  From whom the Agnihotra is duly performed, from whom come the ingredients for
  ablutions, cooking, and libations. May that Kamadhenu, bestower of boons, tranquil,
  partake of this offering presented by me.


  5.  She who delights in the cow-pen, and wanders contentedly in the pastures, with
  body not to be harmed. May that Kamadhenu, bestower of boons, tranquil, partake
  of this offering presented by me.


  6.  Whose sound is in the tones of the bull, and upon whom the ancestors, being
  sustained, are satisfied. May that Kamadhenu, bestower of boons, tranquil, partake
  of this offering presented by me.


  7.  Desiring to remove all faults, I serve her with devotion-filled intellect. May
  that Kamadhenu, bestower of boons, tranquil, partake of this offering presented
  by me.


  8.  Increasing compassion in all, raising high the glory of my lineage. May that
  Kamadhenu, bestower of boons, tranquil, partake of this offering presented by me.'
