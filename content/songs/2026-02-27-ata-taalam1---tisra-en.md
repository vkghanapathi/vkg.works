abstract: A devotional composition in Malahari rāga set to āṭa tāla with tisrajāti
  rhythmic structure, demonstrating the integration of musical notation with Sanskrit
  devotional poetry. The work presents a structured three-part song form (pallavi,
  anupallavi, caraṇam) focused on guru sevā (service to the spiritual teacher) as
  a daily spiritual discipline, accompanied by notated melodic passages and explicit
  philosophical commentary in Sanskrit and English.
preamble: This composition belongs to the Carnatic music tradition of South Indian
  classical music, specifically exemplifying the kīrtana form that synthesizes rāga
  (melodic framework), tāla (rhythmic cycle), and devotional Telugu or Sanskrit text.
  The work emphasizes the Vedantic principle of guru-śiṣya paramparā (teacher-student
  lineage) central to Hindu spiritual practice, presenting guru sevā not merely as
  ritual obligation but as a path to mokṣa (liberation) and divine grace. The explicit
  bilingual commentary (Sanskrit and English) suggests this composition functions
  as both a performable liturgical piece and an educational text for contemporary
  practitioners unfamiliar with classical Sanskrit philosophical vocabulary.

translation_en: "1.  **Bhāvārthaḥ:**\n    Upacharaṇam paricharaṇam guru jana savidhe\n\
  \    Anudinam api karaṇīyam sumudita manasā\n\n    *Meaning:* Service and attendance\
  \ upon the preceptor should be performed daily with a joyful heart.\n\n2.  **Anupallavi:**\n\
  \    Śrī guru pada vī prathimā suvidita garimā\n    Samiṣita sukha mavirata sukha\
  \ miha tava bhavatucha vibhupada mahimā\n\n    *Meaning:* The glory of the lotus\
  \ feet of the revered Guru, whose greatness is well-known, bestows desired happiness\
  \ and unending bliss. May the majesty of the Lord's feet be yours here and hereafter.\n\
  \n3.  **Charanam - Madhyamakālam:**\n    Nahi tu jāmitā viśatu mānase\n    Kvacidapi\
  \ malahara guru paricharite\n    Anuśocana paridevana paritāpana kaṣṭagaṇam\n  \
  \  Kvacidapi malahara guru paricharite mā bhūt\n\n    *Meaning:* Let not weariness\
  \ enter the mind, even slightly, in the service of the Guru, the destroyer of impurities.\
  \ May there be no lamentation, wailing, suffering, or multitude of afflictions,\
  \ even slightly, in the service of the Guru, the destroyer of impurities.\n\n4.\
  \  Api paramaramaṇa mante vasadupa chichariṣu\n    Śiṣya.. guruvara sevā dhuravaha..\n\
  \    Guru padavīṁ guru padavīṁ supadavīṁ supadavīṁ padavīṁ padavīṁ chiraṁ sukhaṁ\
  \ yāsi\n\n    *Meaning:* Even while dwelling in the most delightful abode, O disciple,\
  \ be eager to serve. Bear the burden of service to the excellent Guru. May you attain\
  \ the Guru's state, the Guru's state, the auspicious state, the auspicious state,\
  \ the state, the state, eternally and happily."
