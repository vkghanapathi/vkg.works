  while the reference to the Yajur Veda's mahāyāga (great ritual) and paramēśvara
  (the supreme deity) grounds the work in Vedic ritual and theistic practice. Such
  compositions typically function as vehicles for both spiritual instruction and musical
  cultivation, performed in temples, homes, and learning contexts throughout Karnataka
  and South India.

translation_en: '1.  *Refrain:*

  Practise Dharma as taught by the Vedas; speak the truth always, for your own good.

  Let truth-speaking be your vow; may divine wealth bring you auspiciousness in the
  future.


  2.  In Bhārat, in the assembly of Gurus, may you always be the best among the best.


  (This bhajan was composed during the Yajurveda Mahāyāga in the presence of Parameśvara
  on 7-8-2023.)'
