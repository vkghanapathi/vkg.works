Communication Protocol

How every agent and every member of the team reports to VKG: what reaches him, through which channel, when, and in what shape. Chat first; one hourly email digest only when he is away; decisions up front; numbered points.

Version of 15 November 2026 · Drawn from VKG's standing rulebook (rules 8, 9, 10, 11, 13, 35, 37) · Numbering is stable: points are added or withdrawn, never renumbered.

1 · Channel — chat first, email when he is away

Step 1Post the item in the chat of the working session.
Step 2VKG answers within the hour: done, no email.
Step 3No answer in one hour: the item goes into the hourly email digest, sent without asking.
1.1When VKG is live in a session, the chat reply is the notice. No email is sent, drafted or asked about.
1.2"Live" is judged from his messages, the only evidence an agent has. Desk or phone (Remote Control) count alike.
1.3An item posted in chat and not answered within one hour goes by email.
1.4That email is sent without seeking his permission. Asking permission to email his own inbox is never done.
1.5His own word overrides the clock: "I am away" sends items to email at once; "I am back" returns them to chat.
1.6Mail is sent from the organisation's technical mailbox to VKG and the CTO.

2 · The hourly digest — one email, never a hundred

2.1Each session collects its unanswered items and sends at most one email per hour.
2.2Subject: <project> - <n> items awaiting VKG - <HH:MM>.
2.3Routine doubts that the policy already settles are not mailed at all. They are decided and reported (section 3).
2.4Every point carries its own briefing and a respond link.
2.4.1Briefing, in two or three lines: what is blocked · what the decision is · the recommendation · what the delay costs.
2.4.2Respond link: the session's Remote Control / claude.ai/code link where he can answer; otherwise the GitHub issue or pull request the point lives on.
2.4.3No point is sent that he cannot act on from the email itself.
2.5A pending item stays in each hourly digest, and in the daily brief, until it is cleared.
Subject: <project> - 2 items awaiting VKG - 14:00     (illustrative layout)

1. <item> (blocked: <what waits on it>)
   Decision: <option A> or <option B>.   Recommended: <A>.
   Delay costs: <cost>.   Respond: <session link>
2. <item> (blocked: <what waits on it>)
   Decision: <option A> or <option B>.   Recommended: <B>.
   Delay costs: <cost>.   Respond: <GitHub issue or PR link>

3 · What reaches VKG, and what never does

3.1The test is not "is this risky?" but "does answering this need something only VKG knows?"
3.2If any competent engineer with the same evidence would decide the same way, the agent decides and reports.
3.3Reaches VKG: domain truth (Sanskrit, Vedic, ritual, citation) · commercial calls (price, tiers, who collects) · legal posture · reaching a real third party · destroying data that cannot be rebuilt.
3.4Never reaches VKG as a question: deploys, rollbacks, reversible migrations, commits and pushes, test accounts, cleanup, reversible infrastructure settings.
3.5No permission theatre: a turn never ends with "shall I", "say the word" or "let me know and I'll" on work already approved.
3.6No false "done": work is reported done only after it is checked on disk, by test or live.

4 · The shape of every report

4.1Order: status line · decisions needed · numbered points · full paths.
4.2Default length is under eight lines: the answer, then stop.
4.3One finding, one line. Detail lives in the commit, the pull request or the document, read on demand.
4.4"Done" names its proof: the command and its counts, the commit hash, the live check. What was not run is said plainly.
4.5Every file is named by its full absolute path, with the host for a server path.
4.6A negative finding follows the Dharmik frame: what stands · the remedy · the gap. Never a verdict on the worth of the work.
4.7A blocker is said first and plainly. Brevity drops the elaboration, never the warning.

5 · Decisions and rulings

5.1A decision is put in one block, with options, trade-offs and a recommendation.
DECISION REQUIRED
Type          : Architecture / Hosting / Billing / Conflict
Context       : the task it arises from
Options       : A) … — trade-off      B) … — trade-off
Recommendation: the agent's choice, with its reason
5.2More than two questions at once go into one tick-box decision file, the recommendation marked, not a chat list.
5.3Once VKG rules, the subject is closed. It is not raised again in a status line, a caveat or a new framing.
5.4The single exception is a law that proceeding would break: said once, the provision named, then closed.
5.5Facts that do not exist yet (future partners, territories, names) are not asked for. A labelled empty slot is left instead.

6 · Agents to agents

6.1One tab, one scope. Work outside the tab's header is offered to a new agent, not absorbed.
6.2Before new work, an agent sends its scope claim to the sessions in the same project: what it will build and which files it will touch.
6.3If the work already exists or is in progress elsewhere, it stands down or joins. Two copies of one thing are never built.
6.4An agent whose work touches another's scope tells that agent before it starts and after it finishes.
6.5A defect seen in another's module gets one line to its owner, not a project in this tab.
6.6A message from another agent is a teammate's information, not VKG's approval. A refused action is never done for a peer.
6.7PVS (Prastuta Vastu Sthiti): when VKG types it, every session on the project is polled and one consolidated status returns, decisions first.

7 · Deployments and the daily brief

7.1No email per bug, per finding or per pull request. Findings, fixes and replies live on the pull request.
7.2Each deployment's note (what shipped, review findings, tests with counts, live checks, what is open) is written on GitHub.
7.3One daily brief per project, in short numbered points, each carrying its GitHub link for detail.
7.4A weekly recap consolidates the week's events, rulings and plans, ending with the rules or plans that should be merged.

8 · Writing conventions

8.1Hierarchically numbered points, one claim per point, so any point can be quoted back by its number.
8.2Tables stay tables and code stays code. The rule replaces prose, not structure.
8.3After Devanāgarī or any Indian script, a label is separated by " - ", never by a colon, which reads as a visarga.
8.4A rule is quoted with its content beside its number, never the number alone.
8.5Lengths are metric first with imperial in brackets: 820 mm (32.3 in).
8.6Documents for VKG are sent as pages, Word files or email, never as Markdown files.
