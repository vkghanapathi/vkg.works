About

A life devoted to the Vedas

Brahmasrī Dr. Vamshi Krishna Ghanapāṭhī is a Caturveda Ghanapāṭhī — a master of the highest recitative form of all four Vedas — and a Sanskrit scholar with a PhD and D.Litt to his name. Based in Mysore, Karnataka, he has spent more than three decades in the rigorous study, preservation, and public exposition of the Vedic tradition.

His work spans recitation, ritual (Āgama and Yājña), commentary (Bhāṣya-śāstra), academic research, and prolific authorship. Across temple consecrations, public lectures, broadcast media, and printed volumes, he has carried Vedic scholarship from the chanting hall into contemporary public life.

He is widely regarded for the clarity of his discourse — moving fluently between Sanskrit, Kannada, Telugu, and English — and for his ability to render dense śāstric material accessible without dilution.

"वेदोऽखिलो धर्ममूलम् — the Veda is the root of all dharma. To preserve it whole, to recite it true, and to teach it living: that is the work of a lifetime."
— Dr. Vamshi Krishna Ghanapāṭhī
