About This Study

Every person has a horoscope based on the time and place of birth. Based on this data, the star of the prevalent period is assigned, which is called the Janma Nakshatra (Birth Star).

Since time immemorial, people from the Indian Sub-Continent have attached high importance to the factor of Time in the schedule of events and include Birth Star Compatibility in the background verification of new relationships. Many have reported beneficial results, though this is not quantitatively documented.

In this study, 27 Nakshatras (Luminous Celestial Bodies identified in ancient scriptures) are grouped into 9 categories based on their ruling planets. Compatibility between any two persons is determined by the natural friendship or enmity between their Nakshatra lords — this is called Nakshatra Jaataka Maitri.

How the Survey Works

Step 1 — Register: Provide your birth details (as much as you know). You'll receive a unique participant number.
Step 2 — Relations: List 5–10 important persons in your life and categorise each relationship.
Step 3 — Events: Record major life events and categorise their outcome.
Step 4 — Results: See how traditional predictions compare with your real-life experience.

All fields are optional. Fill in only what you know. Your data is identified by participant number only — names are never displayed publicly.
